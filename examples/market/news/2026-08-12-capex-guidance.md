# Three hyperscalers raise full-year AI capex guidance by 20%

*12 August 2026* — On back-to-back earnings calls this week, the three largest
cloud providers each lifted their 2026 capital-expenditure guidance by roughly
a fifth, citing demand for AI training and inference capacity that continues to
outrun supply. Analysts now expect combined AI infrastructure spending to
exceed 450 billion dollars for the year, with accelerators, advanced packaging
and data-center power identified as the binding constraints.

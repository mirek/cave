`cave serve` draws relation graphs — a store-wide map on the dashboard and at `#/g`, and each entity's 1–3 hop neighborhood on its page — adds a related-entities index, entity-name matches in search, a visited trail and the `/` search shortcut, and folds claim metadata (contexts, tags, comments, timestamps, history/lineage links) behind a per-row disclosure; new `/api/graph` and `/api/names` endpoints and `graph`/`names` view models.

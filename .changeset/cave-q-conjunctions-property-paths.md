CAVE-Q gains conjunctive queries (spec §12.4) — comma-joined premises and `?var` constraints, `OPTIONAL` left-outer groups, `SELECT [DISTINCT]` projection and `ORDER BY` — and property paths: `VERB*`, `(A|B)` alternation, `(A|B)+` and `(A|B)*`. Rule, action and automation premises share the grammar.
